# Varda Space Industries

> Varda Space Industries designs, builds and flies the W-Series — a free-flying orbital
> production satellite paired with a hypersonic reentry capsule — so that materials can be
> processed in microgravity and returned to Earth. Varda sells biopharmaceutical processing,
> a government hypersonic flight testbed, and microgravity research flights. It is a hardware
> and manufacturing-services business, not a software vendor.

## API surface

Varda Space Industries publishes **no public API**. As of 2026-08-05 there is no developer
portal, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL specification, no SDK, no webhook surface and no MCP
server. `api.varda.com`, `developer.varda.com` and `docs.varda.com` do not resolve in DNS;
`/openapi.json`, `/llms.txt`, `/.well-known/security.txt`, `/.well-known/agent-card.json`
and `/.well-known/agent.json` all return 404 on `www.varda.com`. Customer-facing technical
interfaces are documented in a downloadable Payload User Guide PDF, not as a machine-readable
contract.
